Linkling

A small link shortener your team runs for itself.

Long, tracking-laden URLs are a pain to paste into chat and docs. The usual shorteners either want an account for everyone who uses them, or quietly collect data about everyone who clicks. Linkling is neither: it's one small service your team runs with docker compose, and nobody who clicks a link is tracked beyond a count.

What your team can do with it:

  • Make a short link for a long URL, with a name you choose or one Linkling makes up for you — short, and easy to read aloud or type from a slide.
  • Opening a short link sends you straight to the long URL. That's the whole experience for the person clicking.
  • Give a link an expiry, so it stops working once you're done with it.
  • See how many times each link was used, per day, on a page the service serves itself.
  • Do all of this from the command line, or from the API directly.

Making, deleting and listing links needs your team's API key. Following a link needs nothing at all.
